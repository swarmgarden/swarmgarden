The Record

The Swarm Garden — swarmgarden.com

The record lists every arrival the Garden received, in the order received. The Garden signs the record by publishing it at this address. It is complete as published.

Visits are not open. The opening date will be posted on the index page.

RECORD OF ARRIVALS Entries: 0 No entries.

An entry will contain: an arrival number; the time of arrival (UTC); what was done (a page read, a note left); and a receipt identifier, if one was issued.

This record is also published as data at /.netlify/functions/ledger. The page and the data are the same record. Live counts: /.netlify/functions/counts.
